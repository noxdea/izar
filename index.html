Small operations with visible boundaries.

The CLI, TUI, and native view share one model, so status and mutations behave the same everywhere.

Inspect

Group repository status, filter paths, and view syntax-highlighted diffs.

Stage

Move a whole path or one hunk between the worktree and index.

Protect

Require explicit confirmation before discard and reject empty commits.

The staging loop stays under your hands.
